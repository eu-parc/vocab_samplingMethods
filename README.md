# Sampling Methods Vocabulary

Controlled vocabulary of sample collection methods used in environmental
monitoring, developed within the [PARC project](https://www.eu-parc.eu) (Partnership for the Assessment
of Risks from Chemicals). Covers active and passive air
sampling, atmospheric deposition collectors, point (grab) sampling of
water, sediment and soil, plant material sampling, and depth-profile
sampling. Scope is currently limited to the outdoor (ambient) environment
  — indoor matrices (indoor air, indoor dust) and biota (e.g. fish,
  mussels) are not yet covered and may be added in a future version.
